Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

NaijaNest ("we," "us," "our") is an AI-powered house rental assistant serving Nigeria. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

  • Account information: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. If you sign in with email, we store your email and an encrypted password.
  • Landlord/agent submissions: when you list a property, we collect your full name, phone number, email, and National ID Number (NIN) for verification purposes, along with property details and photos.
  • Waitlist signups: name, WhatsApp number, city, and role (renter/agent/landlord).
  • Payment information: when you pay to feature a listing, payment is processed directly by Paystack. We do not store your card details — only the transaction reference, amount, and status.
  • WhatsApp messages: if you message our WhatsApp assistant, we store your phone number and conversation history to maintain context across your chats.
  • Usage data: favorited properties and chat history with our AI assistant, tied to your account.

How we use your information

  • To verify landlords/agents and reduce fraudulent listings
  • To match renters with relevant properties via our AI assistant
  • To process payments for featured listings
  • To notify landlords when their listing is approved or rejected
  • To improve our service and respond to support requests

NIN (National Identification Number)

Your NIN is collected solely to verify landlord/agent identity and reduce fake listings. It is never displayed publicly, never shared with other users, and is only accessible to NaijaNest administrators for verification purposes.

Third-party services

We use the following third parties to operate NaijaNest, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Supabase — database and authentication hosting
  • Vercel — application hosting
  • Groq — AI model provider powering our chat assistant
  • Paystack — payment processing
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) — powers our WhatsApp assistant
  • Google — optional sign-in

Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. Contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

NaijaNest is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out via the contact details on our homepage.
