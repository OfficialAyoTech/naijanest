Paying rent through NaijaNest

  • Payments are processed by Paystack. We never see or store your card details.
  • When you pay through NaijaNest, your payment is held for a short holding period (currently 24 hours) before it is released to the payee. You can confirm move-in to release it sooner, or report a problem within the holding period to pause the release while we review it.
  • Who is paid depends on the listing. By default rent goes to the landlord. Where an agent or other lister acts for the landlord, the listing shows whether rent goes to the landlord with fees to the lister, or whether the lister collects everything. Read the payment notice on the listing before you pay.
  • The total you pay is shown before you pay and may include the agency, documentation and caution fees set by the lister, and a NaijaNest platform fee if one applies. Caution fees are the lister's and are refundable or forfeitable according to the tenancy terms agreed between you and the landlord.
  • Payment release times depend on our payment rules and on Paystack and bank processing. Delays outside our control can happen.
Only pay through NaijaNest's "Pay Rent Securely" button. If you send money to a landlord or agent directly, in cash or by transfer, NaijaNest can't see it, protect it or help you recover it. Never pay anyone who asks you to pay outside the app.
